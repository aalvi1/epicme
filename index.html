How rewards work

You earn XP for adding meaningful residents, recording observations, and noting insights. Daily reflection earns a small bonus once per calendar day. Achievements are awarded only once.

A difficult day can be worth reflecting on, too. The game doesn't judge whether your emotions or responses are “good.”

Privacy & your data

This is an offline, single-file prototype. Entries are saved to this browser's local storage only. There is no account, synchronization, analytics, AI processing, or server connection.

Browser storage can be cleared by the browser or device. Export a backup if you want to keep your progress. Import replaces the current progress after confirmation.
