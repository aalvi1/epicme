How progress works

You earn XP for experiencing meaningful elements, recording observations, and noting insights. Daily reflection earns a small bonus once per calendar day. Achievements are awarded only once.

A difficult day can be worth reflecting on, too. The game doesn't judge whether your emotions or responses are “good.”

Privacy & your data

This is a single-file prototype. Entries are saved in this browser's local storage. There is no account, synchronization, analytics, or AI processing. When hosted on GitHub Pages, the page itself is delivered over the web.

Browser storage can be cleared by the browser or device. Export a backup if you want to keep your progress. Import replaces the current progress after confirmation.
